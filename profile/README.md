# Mountgram

Recent AI experiments from [Topper Bowers](https://github.com/tobowers).

Mountgram is a small public surface for work around AI systems that feel immediate: memory, tools, agents, multimodal perception, spatial media, and software that can respond to context instead of just producing text.

## Recent Experiments

Projects here are likely to be rough, specific, and useful before they are polished.

- Agents that can use tools and remember what matters.
- Interfaces for inspecting and editing structured knowledge.
- Multimodal workflows for images, voice, and spatial material.
- Small prototypes that test whether an AI workflow is actually worth building.

## What This Is Not

- Not a product launch.
- Not a consulting funnel.
- Not a pile of prompt tricks.
